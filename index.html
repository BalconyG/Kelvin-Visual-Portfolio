關於我

黃庚鴻 Kelvin

保持好奇，以不同的視覺語言探索故事。

你好，我是黃庚鴻 Kelvin，一位對視覺敘事、影像創作、平面設計及攝影充滿興趣的跨媒介創作者。

對我而言，創作並不局限於某一種媒介。短片、攝影作品或平面設計，都能以不同的方式傳遞想法與情感。我喜歡探索不同的視覺表達形式，嘗試各種創作可能，並思考如何將腦海中的想法轉化為能夠觸動他人的作品。

我的創作過程始於好奇心，也建立在不斷探索與嘗試之上。從最初的構思、概念發展，到實際執行與細節調整，我享受創作過程中的每一個階段。我相信，每一個項目都是一次學習的機會，讓我能夠從不同角度觀察事物，並發掘新的可能性。

在持續探索與成長的過程中，我希望創作出不只是具有視覺吸引力，同時也帶有思考、情感與意義的作品。

保持好奇，不斷探索，持續創作。

About Me

黄庚鴻 Kelvin Wong

A curious mind, exploring stories through different visual languages.

I am Kelvin Wong, a multidisciplinary creative with an interest in visual storytelling, moving images, graphic design and photography.

To me, creativity is not confined to a single medium. A film, a photograph or a piece of graphic design can each communicate an idea in a different way. I enjoy exploring these different forms of expression, experimenting with visual elements and finding meaningful ways to turn ideas into something people can see, feel and connect with.

My creative process is driven by curiosity and a willingness to explore. I value the journey behind each project — from developing an initial concept to working through the details and bringing an idea to life. I believe every project is an opportunity to learn, observe and see things from a new perspective.

As I continue to grow and explore, I hope to create work that is not only visually engaging, but also thoughtful, expressive and meaningful.

Always exploring. Always creating.
